Hoops Rotation

Who plays when, and where.

How it works
  1. Set the clock. The game is cut into equal shifts: each period is split at the sub points. 4 quarters of 8 minutes with 1 sub per quarter makes 8 shifts of 4:00. Lineups change only between shifts.
  2. Fill in the roster. For each player: the share of the game they should play, the positions they can play (1–5), and whether they start and close. Mark who's not at the game as Out.
  3. Percents are scaled to 500%, because five players are on the floor. Nobody goes over 100%.
  4. Make rotation. Every shift has exactly one player at each position. The starters play the first shift and the closers the last. Minutes come in whole shifts, so they land close to the targets, not on them. A planned time in amber is off by a full shift or more, because of positions, starters or closers.
  5. Adjust. Tap a name in the lineup chart to put in someone from the bench or swap positions. Try another arrangement shuffles who plays when, keeping everyone's minutes.
