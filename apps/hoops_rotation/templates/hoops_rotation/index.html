Hoops Rotation

Who plays when, and where.

How it works
  1. Set the game and the sub rules. The planner picks when to sub, on the minute (or half minute). You set how many players can come in at one sub, how long a player stays in once they're in, and how long the closing five play at the end. Any number can change at a period break.
  2. Fill in the roster. For each player: the share of the game they should play, every position they can play (1–5), and whether they start and close. Mark who's not at the game as Out.
  3. Percents are scaled to 500%, because five players are on the floor. Nobody goes over 100%.
  4. Make rotation. At every moment there's exactly one player at each position. Nobody sits half the game in a row, and nobody misses more than one whole quarter (players at 0% excepted). Within those rules the plan keeps minutes close to target and sits short, and leans toward more subs over long sits. Sub times are targets: sub at the first dead ball after.
  5. Adjust. Tap a name in the lineup chart to put in someone from the bench or swap positions. If a change breaks one of your rules, the page says which. Try another arrangement makes a different plan from the same inputs.
