🍳 Cooking Timer

How it works
  1. Add each dish and its steps. A step is what to do and how many minutes after the previous step to do it. The first step counts from when the dish goes on.
    Chicken: after 15 min → Flip, then after 25 min → Take out
  2. Choose the timing. Start together starts every dish now. Finish together tells you when to start each dish so they're all ready at once: as soon as possible, or by a time you pick.
  3. Tap Start Cooking. Each dish shows what to do next and counts down to it.
  4. When the alarm rings, do the step and tap Done. It keeps ringing until you do. The next step's timer starts from your tap, so being a bit late never shortens it.
  5. Keep this page open with the screen on. Alarms can't ring if the phone locks or you switch to another app. On iPhone the silent switch mutes the alarm, so use ▶ Test at the bottom to check.
